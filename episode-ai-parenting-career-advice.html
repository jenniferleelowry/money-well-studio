Season 2 • Episode 13

AI Series, Part 4: The Old Career Advice Has Expired: What Parents Should Tell Kids About AI

June 19, 2026 • 31 min

What parents should tell high school and college-age kids about AI, education, career preparation, adaptability and the skills that may matter most in a changing economy.

Listen on Spotify Listen on Apple Podcasts

About This Episode

In the final installment of the AI and work series, Jennifer and Julie bring the conversation home to parents and students. The old formula of good grades, a degree and a stable profession is no longer enough by itself. The goal is not to find one perfectly AI-proof major. It is to help young adults become adaptable, AI-literate and capable of combining human judgment with technical fluency.

In This Episode

  • Why parents are reasonable to feel anxious about AI and the future of work
  • Why the old 'get a degree and you'll be set' advice needs an update
  • How students can use AI tools without becoming dependent on them
  • Why critical thinking, communication, verification and adaptability matter across majors
  • Fields students may want to explore, from data and cybersecurity to healthcare, education, skilled trades and creative work
  • Why the strongest future careers may combine human expertise with technology rather than choosing one over the other
