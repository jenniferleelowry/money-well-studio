Dry January, Messy Resolutions & the Art of the Reset

January 16, 2026 • 21 min

Dry January, failed resolutions, habit formation and a more forgiving way to reset routines without confusing perfection with progress.

Listen on Spotify Listen on Apple Podcasts

About This Episode

January arrives with plans, intentions and a suspicious amount of confidence. Then real life intervenes. Jennifer and Julie check in on their own resolutions, unpack the origins of Dry January and talk about why habit replacement and mindfulness can work better than rigid all-or-nothing rules.

In This Episode

  • Why resolutions often feel easier on January 1 than January 16
  • Where Dry January came from and why it was not designed as punishment
  • How social habits can shape drinking without deliberate choices
  • Why simply removing a habit can leave a vacuum
  • How replacement behaviors can make change easier to sustain
  • Why a reset can still count even when the original plan gets messy
