Season 2 • Episode 4

The Economics of Love: Why Valentine's Day Makes Us Spend, Stress, and Sometimes Break Up

February 13, 2026 • 26 min

Why Valentine’s Day spending feels different, how commercialization and social expectations shape money decisions, and why financial communication matters more than performative romance.

Listen on Spotify Listen on Apple Podcasts

About This Episode

Valentine’s Day is romantic, expensive and surprisingly revealing. Jennifer and Julie unpack how a holiday built around love became a case study in commercialization, social expectations, spending pressure and relationship tension, including the phenomenon sometimes called Red Tuesday.

In This Episode

  • How Valentine’s Day became a major commercial holiday
  • Why symbolic spending can feel different from ordinary purchases
  • How cultural expectations quietly influence financial behavior
  • Why trying to save money without communicating can backfire
  • What Red Tuesday says about relationship pressure around the holiday
  • How couples can focus on meaning rather than financial performance
