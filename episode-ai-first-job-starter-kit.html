AI Series, Part 3: Your AI Starter Kit for Your First Real Job

June 5, 2026 • 30 min

A practical AI and career starter kit for new graduates and early-career workers navigating a tighter job market and changing entry-level work.

Listen on Spotify Listen on Apple Podcasts

About This Episode

Getting your first real job has always been stressful, but AI is changing many of the starter tasks new employees once used to learn the ropes. Jennifer and Julie look at how new graduates can get hired, get noticed and grow when first drafts, research, summaries, basic analysis, emails, decks and even code can increasingly be assisted by AI.

In This Episode

  • Why the entry-level job market feels especially difficult for recent graduates
  • How AI is changing traditional starter tasks
  • Why AI readiness matters even if AI is not your field of study
  • Why critical thinking becomes more valuable when polished first drafts are easy to generate
  • The enduring value of communication, relationships, follow-through and usefulness
  • How to learn where AI fits inside your actual profession rather than treating AI as a standalone career

Cocktail of the Episode

The Classic Martini

The Classic Martini keeps things simple: gin, dry vermouth and either an olive or lemon twist. Stir it with ice until cold, strain, and serve it with the confidence of someone who knows the first draft is not the final answer.
