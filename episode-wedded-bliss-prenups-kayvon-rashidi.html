Wedded Bliss: What Happens to Yours When It Becomes Ours?

September 14, 2026 • 29 min

Family law attorney and mediator Kayvon Rashidi joins Jennifer and Julie for a practical conversation about prenups, postnups, property, inheritance, business ownership and the financial expectations couples should talk through before marriage.

Listen on Spotify Listen on Apple Podcasts

About This Episode

You already have a prenup. The question is whether you chose the terms. In Part 2 of our conversation on prenuptial agreements, Jennifer and Julie go deeper with special guest Kayvon Rashidi, a board-certified family law attorney and mediator based in Austin, Texas.

Kayvon explains who should consider a prenup, what a prenup can and cannot do, and when couples should start talking about money and financial expectations. He also brings a practical perspective to postnups, property, inheritance and the financial decisions couples should think through before marriage.

In This Episode

  • Why a prenup is not just for the ultra-wealthy
  • How second marriages and children from previous relationships can change the conversation
  • Why business ownership, future business growth, real estate and inheritance can matter
  • What a prenup can and cannot do
  • Why each person should have their own attorney
  • The importance of financial disclosure before signing an agreement
  • How wills, trusts, postnuptial agreements and moving to another state can fit into the broader plan

The Takeaway

A prenup is not necessarily a prediction that a marriage will fail. It can be a way for couples to talk openly about money, ownership, inheritance, businesses, children and the financial expectations they are bringing into a marriage.
