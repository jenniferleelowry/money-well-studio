Fair Share for the Ladies - Part 2 (With Special Guest: Vasu Reddy from National Women's Law Center)

March 13, 2026 • 30 min

Vasu Reddy of the National Women’s Law Center joins Money Well Studio to explain what the gender pay gap measures, why it persists and how policy can affect women’s earnings.

Listen on Spotify Listen on Apple Podcasts

About This Episode

Part 2 moves from history to the policy architecture behind women’s earnings. Joined by Vasu Reddy of the National Women’s Law Center, Jennifer and Julie examine what the pay-gap number actually measures, why confusion around it persists and which structural forces matter most for women’s long-term financial security.

In This Episode

  • What the gender pay gap does and does not measure
  • Why occupational segregation continues to matter
  • How caregiving penalties can compound across a career
  • Why simply telling women to negotiate does not address the entire problem
  • How pay transparency policies can change incentives
  • Why public policy can shape lifetime earnings and retirement security
