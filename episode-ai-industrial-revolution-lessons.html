Season 2 • Episode 10

AI Series, Part 1: Who Survives a Revolution? Lessons for the Age of AI

May 8, 2026 • 26 min

What the Industrial Revolutions, Luddites, productivity gains and earlier waves of technological displacement can teach us about AI and the future of work.

Listen on Spotify Listen on Apple Podcasts

About This Episode

AI feels new, but the fear and disruption around technological change are not. Jennifer and Julie go back through the Industrial Revolutions to examine how technology has repeatedly changed jobs, industries and communities, often creating progress for the economy while imposing real costs on particular workers.

In This Episode

  • Why the Luddites were not simply anti-technology
  • How the power loom and automated flour mills changed work
  • How the cotton gin changed incentives with consequences far beyond productivity
  • Why productivity gains do not automatically translate into better outcomes for every worker
  • What Alexis de Tocqueville observed about wealth and poverty existing side by side
  • How historical technology transitions can help frame the real questions around AI: who adapts, who benefits and who bears the cost
