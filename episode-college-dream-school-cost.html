Season 2 • Episode 17

College Series: The Real Cost of Choosing the “Dream School”

August 17, 2026 • 26 min

How families can compare college costs, net price, graduation rates, debt and outcomes before deciding whether a dream school is worth the price.

Listen on Spotify Listen on Apple Podcasts

About This Episode

The acceptance letter arrives. Your kid loves the campus. Everyone is excited. Then comes the harder question: is this school worth this price for this student? Jennifer and Julie take a numbers-first look at one of the biggest financial decisions many families will ever make.

In This Episode

  • Why sticker price is only the beginning of a college-cost comparison
  • How to use net price, financial aid, graduation rates and student debt together
  • Why earnings and outcomes by field of study can matter more than a school’s overall reputation
  • How families can separate the emotional appeal of a dream school from the financial tradeoffs
  • Why every serious college contender deserves a look at its own Net Price Calculator
